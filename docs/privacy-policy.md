# PromptForge AI Privacy Policy

This is a technical starter draft and must be reviewed and completed before publication.

PromptForge AI may process text entered by a user to generate or improve prompts. When remote generation is enabled, the text is sent to the configured PromptForge backend and the AI provider used by that backend. Saved prompts and usage counters may be stored locally on the device.

The final public policy should state exact retention periods, analytics/crash services, subprocessors, account data, deletion requests, payment handling, and contact details actually used.
